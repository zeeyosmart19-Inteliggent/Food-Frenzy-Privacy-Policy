Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: Food Frenzies Clicker does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game works completely offline, and everything you do in it stays on your own device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Food Frenzies Clicker (the “App”) handles information. The App is a 3D food clicker game for iPhone, iPad and Mac, created and owned by ZEEYO (“we”, “us”, “our”). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1.Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information or usage data from the App. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any contact details;
  • your location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers or IP addresses;
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports through any third-party service.

The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). It does not connect to the internet: the game, its graphics, sounds and font are all included inside the App.

2.Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the App stores your game data only on your device, inside the App's private storage. This includes your coins, collected foods, upgrades, achievements, settings, play statistics, and the chef name and avatar you choose in the game. A backup copy is kept inside the App so your progress survives App updates.

This information never leaves your device through the App, and we cannot see or access it.

  • Device backups: if you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your game data is included like other app data. Those backups are provided by Apple and controlled by you. We have no access to them.
  • Deleting your data: use Settings › Reset progress in the game, or delete the App from your device. Either removes your game data from the device.

3.Sharing a save code

In the game's Settings you can export a “save code” to back up or move your progress. The code is created on your device and shown in the standard iOS share sheet and clipboard. It goes only where you choose to send it, for example Notes, Messages or Files. We never receive it.

4.Diagnostics provided by Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics through App Store Connect. This is controlled entirely by you and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not use this information to identify anyone.

5.Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Parents can contact us at any time with questions.

6.Third-party services

The App contains no third-party services that collect data. The only third-party component included is the Fredoka font, which is a file inside the App, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It does not collect or send any information.

7.Security

Your game data is stored in the App's private, sandboxed storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It is protected by the security of your device, including your passcode.

8.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union and United Kingdom, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or sell. All App data stays on your device under your control (see section 2).
